Tips
• Don't pair the piano in Android's Bluetooth settings. Connect from this page instead.
• Close the Roland Piano App first. Only one app can be connected to the piano at a time.
• "All sounds" plays from the phone, so plug in a speaker for big sound. Wired has less delay than Bluetooth.
